# Expert Note Landing Page Design Handoff

## Purpose
Design a landing page with integrated login for Expert Note - a knowledge capture system that helps organizations preserve expert judgment in the AI era.

---

## The Core Problem Expert Note Solves

**In the AI era, generation is abundant but evaluation is scarce.**

- AI can produce infinite ideas, variations, and content
- But AI cannot reliably determine what's valuable or what will work in practice
- Expert judgment comes from years of mental simulation and experience
- This tacit knowledge is being lost as experienced professionals retire
- 70% of organizational knowledge is unwritten and locked inside expert heads

---

## Value Proposition

**"Your Expertise. Preserved. Amplified."**

Expert Note transforms tacit expert knowledge into structured, reusable AI assets.

**Key Benefits:**
1. Capture what experts know before it's lost
2. Convert intuitive judgment into AI-powered decision systems
3. Scale expert-level evaluation across teams
4. Create "AI that thinks like you"

---

## How It Works (4-Step Pipeline)

```
Documents → Annotations → Knowledge → AI Prompts
```

### Step 1: Upload Expert Documents
Users upload PDFs, Word docs, notes, or create directly in the system. These are the raw materials containing expert insights.

### Step 2: Add Structured Annotations
Experts mark up their documents with three levels of insight:

| Level | Color | What It Captures |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| **MACRO** | Red | High-level principles and frameworks |
| **MESO** | Yellow | Patterns and evidence structures |
| **MICRO** | Green | Specific details, edits, examples |

### Step 3: AI Extracts Knowledge
The system analyzes annotations and extracts structured knowledge - generalizing context-specific insights into reusable patterns.

### Step 4: Generate AI Prompts
Users create production-ready AI system prompts that embody their expertise - "AI that thinks like you" for use with Claude, Cursor, or other tools.

## Who Uses Expert Note

- **Academic Researchers** - Annotate papers, generate peer-review prompts
- **Teachers & Advisors** - Capture feedback patterns, generate grading rubrics
- **Professional Services** - Preserve consulting and evaluation frameworks
- **Teams** - Share expert knowledge across organizations

## Key Features to Highlight

### For Individuals
- Document management (upload PDF, Word, markdown, plain text)
- Three-level annotation system with visual color coding
- AI-powered knowledge extraction
- Prompt generation from your knowledge base

### For Teams
- Organization-based sharing and collaboration
- Role-based permissions (view-only or edit)
- Shared knowledge base across team members
- Invitation system for team onboarding

## Landing Page Requirements

### Must Include
1. **Login Form** - Username/password fields with login button
2. **Clear value proposition** - What Expert Note does and why it matters
3. **Simple workflow visualization** - The 4-step pipeline
4. **Target users** - Who benefits from this tool

### Tone
- Professional but not corporate
- Intellectual depth without being academic
- Clean, modern, focused

### Keep It Simple
This is a login page with context, not a full marketing site. Users arriving here need to:
1. Understand what Expert Note does (quickly)
2. Log in to access the system
