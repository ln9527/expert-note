2. Annotate with Three Levels

Mark important content using a structured 3-level system that creates hierarchy AI can understand

🔴
MACRO
Keyboard: Cmd+1

What to mark: Big ideas, themes, frameworks, research questions

"Machine learning requires three key components: data, algorithms, and compute power..."
🟡
MESO
Keyboard: Cmd+2

What to mark: Supporting evidence, methodologies, secondary themes

"The training process uses backpropagation to adjust neural network weights..."
🟢
MICRO
Keyboard: Cmd+3

What to mark: Specific facts, quotes, data points, citations

"Model accuracy improved from 87% to 94% after optimization"

Annotation Interface

Essential Tips & Best Practices

✅ DO
  • Start with MACRO - identify big ideas first
  • Be selective - annotate what's important
  • Keep annotations focused - one concept each
  • Use consistent criteria across documents
  • Choose the right extraction guide for your domain
  • Tag strategically (domain, type, status, project)
❌ DON'T
  • Annotate every sentence
  • Mix multiple ideas in one annotation
  • Skip the hierarchy - all three levels matter
  • Extract knowledge without annotations
  • Create tags without a naming convention
  • Focus on quantity over quality
