# Getting Started with Expert Note

This guide will help you get up and running with Expert Note, an annotation-based knowledge capture system designed for structured expert note-taking.

---

## What is Expert Note?

Expert Note is a knowledge management system that helps you:

- **Capture documents** - Upload and organize markdown documents, PDFs, and Word files
- **Annotate with precision** - Add structured annotations at three levels (Macro, Meso, Micro)
- **Extract knowledge** - Use AI to transform your annotations into organized knowledge entries
- **Generate prompts** - Create AI system prompts based on your extracted knowledge

The system is built around organizations, allowing teams to collaborate on shared content while maintaining individual ownership of documents and knowledge.

---

## Logging In

### Accessing the System

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the Expert Note URL provided by your administrator
2. You will see the login page with the Expert Note title

### Sign In Steps

1. Enter your **Username** in the first field
2. Enter your **Password** in the second field
3. Click the **Sign In** button

If your credentials are correct, you will be redirected to your dashboard. If you see an error message:
- Double-check your username and password for typos
- Ensure your account has been activated by your administrator
- Contact your organization owner or system administrator if problems persist

### First-Time Users

If you do not have an account yet, click the **Register with invitation code** link below the sign-in form to create a new account.

---

## Registration

New users must register using an invitation code. This ensures that only authorized individuals can access the system.

### Prerequisites

Before registering, you will need:
- An **invitation code** from your organization administrator or system administrator
- A web browser with internet access

### Registration Steps

1. Navigate to the login page and click **Register with invitation code**

2. Fill in the registration form:

   | Field | Required | Description |
   |-------|----------|-------------|
   | **Invitation Code** | Yes | The code provided by your administrator (case-insensitive) |
   | **Username** | Yes | Your unique login name (minimum 3 characters) |
   | **Display Name** | No | How your name appears to other users |
   | **Phone Number** | No | Your contact phone number |
   | **Email** | No | Your email address for notifications |
   | **Password** | Yes | Your account password (minimum 8 characters) |
   | **Confirm Password** | Yes | Re-enter your password to confirm |

3. Click **Create Account**

4. Upon successful registration, you will be automatically logged in and redirected to your dashboard

### Important Notes

- Your username cannot be changed after registration
- Choose a strong password that you can remember
- The invitation code determines your role and organization membership
- If you receive an error about the invitation code, verify it with the person who provided it

## Understanding Your Role

Expert Note uses a role-based permission system. Your role determines what features you can access and what actions you can perform.

### Organization Owner

As an **Organization Owner**, you have full administrative access within your organization:

- **Create and manage documents** - Upload, edit, and delete your own documents
- **Share content** - Control sharing settings for your documents and knowledge entries
- **Manage organization members** - View member list and manage member accounts
- **Create invitation codes** - Generate codes to invite new members to your organization
- **Access all shared content** - View and (if permitted) edit content shared by members
- **Manage tags** - Create and organize tags for categorizing content

### Organization Member

As an **Organization Member**, you have access to collaborative features:

- **Create your own content** - Upload and manage your own documents and knowledge entries
- **View shared content** - Access documents and knowledge shared by your organization
- **Edit shared content** - Modify shared content when edit permission is granted
- **Use knowledge extraction** - Transform annotations into knowledge entries
- **Generate prompts** - Create AI prompts based on available knowledge

### Key Differences

| Capability | Owner | Member |
|------------|-------|--------|
| Create own documents | Yes | Yes |
| View shared content | Yes | Yes |
| Edit shared content (when allowed) | Yes | Yes |
| Manage organization members | Yes | No |
| Create invitation codes | Yes | No |
| Access Settings > Members | Yes | No |
| Access Settings > Invites | Yes | No |

## Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you will see the main dashboard. This is your central hub for accessing all Expert Note features.

### Navigation Bar

The navigation bar at the top of the screen provides quick access to main sections:

| Link | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Expert Note** (logo) | Returns to the Documents dashboard |
| **Knowledge Base** | View and manage extracted knowledge entries |
| **Prompts** | Create and manage AI system prompts |
| **Settings** | Access account settings, tags, and administration |
| **Logout** | Sign out of your account |

Your display name (or username) appears in the top right corner to confirm you are logged in.

### Documents Dashboard

The main dashboard displays your documents in a table format:

- **Title** - The document name (click to open)
- **Status** - Current state: Raw, Annotated, or Refined
- **Annotations** - Count of Macro, Meso, and Micro annotations
- **Tags** - Category labels assigned to the document
- **Uploaded By** - Who created the document
- **Updated** - When the document was last modified
- **Sharing** - Whether the document is shared with your organization
- **Edit** - Whether shared users can edit (owners only see this for their documents)
- **Actions** - Download and delete options

### Creating a New Document

Click the **+ New Document** button in the top right to:
- Type or paste markdown content directly
- Upload files (Markdown, PDF, or DOCX)

### Search and Filters

The filter bar below the page title helps you find specific documents:

- **Search** - Type to filter by document title
- **Tags** - Filter by one or more tags
- **Status** - Show only Raw, Annotated, or Refined documents
- **User** - Filter by document creator

Click any active filter to remove it, or use **Clear All** to reset all filters.

### Statistics Panel

At the bottom of the dashboard, three statistics cards summarize your workspace:

- **Total Documents** - Number of documents in your current view
- **Annotated** - Documents with status Annotated or Refined
- **Total Annotations** - Sum of all Macro, Meso, and Micro annotations
