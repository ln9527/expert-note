Academic Supervision & Grading

学术指导与评分

Problem 问题

Professors repeat the same feedback 100 times. AI graders are too generic.

教授重复相同的反馈 100 次。AI 评分又太笼统。

Solution 方案

Annotate 5 papers. The system learns your specific criteria for "strong argumentation" and creates a "Pre-grading Agent" for students.

批注 5 篇论文。系统学习您对“有力论证”的具体标准，并为学生创建一个“预评分 Agent”。

Legal Contract Review

法律合同审查

Problem 问题

Junior associates miss subtle risks. Senior partners don't have time to review every draft.

初级律师会漏掉细微风险。高级合伙人没时间审查每一份草稿。

Solution 方案

Partners annotate why a clause is risky. The system builds a "Firm Policy Agent" that flags risks based on partner intuition, not just keywords.

合伙人批注为什么某条款有风险。系统构建一个“律所政策 Agent”，基于合伙人的直觉而非关键词来标记风险。

Personalized Writing Coach

个性化写作教练

Problem 问题

Generic AI writing sounds robotic and bland.

通用 AI 写作听起来机械且平淡。

Solution 方案

Annotate your own writing to highlight your "voice"—phrase choices, tone shifts. Create an AI that writes exactly like you.

批注您自己的文章以突出您的“声音”——用词选择、语气转换。创建一个写起来完全像您的 AI。
